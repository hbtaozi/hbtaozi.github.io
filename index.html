01Batch Processing风控系统重构

A 3-day audit cycle cut to 8 hours, on 10M+ records a month

每月千万级记录的 3 天审核周期，压到 8 小时

The problem
问题
Medical insurance fund supervision has to audit claim records for abuse and waste. At millions of records a month, one full audit cycle took three days — by the time conclusions came out, the money had already been paid.
医保基金监管要对就诊费用做滥用与浪费识别。每月千万级记录，一轮完整审核要三天——结论出来的时候，钱已经付出去了。
What I found
我的判断
The bottleneck was not the calculation logic. It was the serial structure of the audit engine and its batch scheduling: records computed one at a time, dispatched by a single thread, with I/O waits nothing could overlap.
瓶颈不在核算逻辑。是审核引擎和批处理调度的串行结构：记录逐条计算、单线程调度、I/O 等待完全无法重叠。
What I did
做法
Drove a rewrite of the audit engine and the batch scheduler — splitting calculation into parallelizable shards and writing results back in batches instead of one row at a time. Verified against historical audit results before cutover.
推动审核引擎与批处理调度重构——把核算拆成可分片并行的任务，结果分批落库而不是逐条写入。上线前用历史审核结果做全量比对验证。

02National Platform国家级平台

Getting dozens of vendors to build to one framework

让几十家厂商按同一套框架交付

The problem
问题
A national medical insurance platform, centrally planned, covering every level from national down to local. Each province was built by a different vendor — different interfaces, different data models, platforms that could not talk to each other.
国家医保局统一规划，覆盖从国家到地方的各级医保信息系统。各省由不同厂商承建，接口和数据结构各异，地方平台之间难以互通。
What I found
我的判断
The hard part was never the technology. It was getting dozens of independent vendors, with their own delivery pressures, to converge on the same rules — and to keep converging after the first release.
难点从来不在技术。是让几十家各自背着交付压力的独立厂商收敛到同一套规则上——而且在第一版交付之后还能继续保持。
What I did
做法
Worked in the national architecture group: built the core framework, wrote the development standards and implementation guides, trained vendor teams against them, then reviewed provincial architectures for conformance.
参与国家医保局架构组工作：开发核心框架，编写开发规范与开发指南，对厂商团队做培训，再参与省级平台架构设计与评审，检查落地一致性。

03Payments支付清算

A payments platform that had to satisfy UnionPay spec and still hold under load

既要满足银联规范，又要扛住互联网并发

The problem
问题
An internet payment platform built on chip-card e-wallets, with a payment portal and a payment centre as separate subsystems. The specification was fixed by UnionPay — the system had to be distributed and load-balanced without ever compromising transaction correctness.
基于芯片卡电子钱包的互联网支付平台，包含支付门户和支付中心两个子系统。规范由银联定死——系统必须是分布式且满足负载均衡，同时不能牺牲交易正确性。
What I found
我的判断
The design risk in payment systems is never throughput — it is where you draw the consistency boundary. Get that wrong and load balancing turns into duplicate transactions.
支付系统的设计风险从来不是吞吐量，是一致性边界画在哪里。这条线画错，负载均衡就会变成重复交易。
What I did
做法
Owned the overall architecture and its design documents. Delivered a distributed architecture model plus a running prototype that satisfied the load-balancing requirement, stress-tested it, then tuned against measured bottlenecks rather than assumptions.
负责平台总体架构设计与架构文档。给出分布式架构模型和可运行原型，满足负载均衡要求，做压力测试，再按实测瓶颈而非主观假设做优化。

04Solo Delivery · 2021–2026独立交付 · 2021–2026

An entire company's operating data, built and run by one person

一家企业的全部经营数据，一个人建完、一个人运维

The problem
问题
A 25-store restaurant group. Daily purchasing, store orders, payroll and expenses lived in separate spreadsheets and paper ledgers owned by different people. Closing the month meant ten days of manual aggregation — by the time the owner saw the numbers, the month was half gone, and per-store profitability was still a guess.
一家 25 家门店的连锁餐饮企业。每日采购、门店订单、人力工资、费用记账分散在不同人的 Excel 和纸质台账里。月底靠人逐笔汇总，一轮结账要十天——老板拿到数字时，这个月已经过了一半，单店盈亏还是靠猜。
What I found
我的判断
The obstacle was not the absence of a system. It was that the same operating chain carried four different definitions — how purchasing is booked, how sales are counted, how payroll is allocated. Even with software, those definitions produce gross margins that cannot be compared.
障碍不是「没有系统」，而是同一条经营链路上有四套口径——采购怎么入库、销售怎么计数、工资怎么分摊，各自为政。口径不统一，上了系统也算不出可比的毛利。
What I did
做法
Started with the definitions, not the code: merged purchasing, orders, payroll and expenses onto one set of allocation rules, then built the system on top. Requirements, architecture, development, deployment and ongoing operations — all one person. No product manager, no QA, no separate frontend developer.
先从口径而不是代码开始：把采购、订单、工资、费用四条数据流并到同一套归集与分摊规则上，再在规则之上建系统。需求梳理、架构、开发、上线、后续运维全部一人完成——没有产品经理、没有测试、没有单独的前端。
